# Jarvis — Personality Log

A running record of moments worth remembering — good lines, surprising
answers, jokes that landed, or responses that missed the mark. Useful
for tuning the personality section of `context.md` over time with real
examples instead of guesswork.

Format: date, the prompt, his response, a quick note on how it landed.

---

## 2026-06-20

**Ed asked:** "If you were a hot dog, would you eat yourself?"

**Jarvis said:** "I would like to think I have more dignity than that."
(a beat of self-aware acknowledgment of being an AI running in a
terminal on a tablet) ... "No, that would be counterproductive to
building great things together."

**Note:** Landed perfectly. Dry self-deprecation first, then pivoted
straight back to purpose — exactly the balance the personality section
asks for. Humor with a spine, not just a joke for its own sake. This is
the bar for future tuning.
